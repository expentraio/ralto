What to look for: scroll your eye down each column quickly. In the left-stripe version, client colour reads as a continuous rail the whole way down — BBC/Man Utd/Man City are identifiable before you've even read a word. In the bottom-stripe version, each stripe sits flush against the next card's top edge, so adjacent cards' colours can blur together at a glance, and the identifying colour only appears once you've already scanned past the title.
